# 💎 Armory & Marketplace / 武库与集市系统

## Case Unboxing & Quality Hierarchy / 武器箱与品质阶梯

RenYing features a cosmetic skin and weapon system inspired by competitive item economies. **No real money is used**; all items are unlocked using in-game **Ink (墨锭)** earned through matches and achievements.

### Quality Tiers (品质分类)
From entry-tier to ultra-rare:
1. **军规级 (Mil-Spec / Blue)** — 53% drop rate
2. **受限 (Restricted / Purple)** — 27% drop rate
3. **保密 (Classified / Pink)** — 13% drop rate
4. **隐秘 (Covert / Red)** — 5.2% drop rate
5. **非凡 (Extraordinary / Gold)** — 1.8% drop rate

### StatTrak Editions (斩数版)
- Approximately 20% of unboxed skins drop as **斩数版 (StatTrak)**.
- Tracks player kill counts and displays glowing callouts on inspect and victory screens.

---

## Trade-Up Contracts (汰换合同)
- Place 10 collectibles of the same quality tier into the contract.
- Produces 1 collectible of the next higher quality tier.

## Player Marketplace (玩家集市)
- Players can list collectibles on the community market for other players to buy with Ink.
- 15% transaction fee is recycled from the economy to prevent inflation.
- Safe transactions protected by Cloudflare Worker D1 atomic operations.
