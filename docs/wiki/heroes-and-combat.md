## Hero Notes / 侠客要点

- **青锋 Qing Feng** — the all-rounder. Builds meter faster than anyone. Gale Slash dashes through the enemy and resets Sword Qi, so the two chain together.
- **绯影 Fei Ying** — the fastest hero, with a light frame. Wins with speed, backstabs and Shadow Blink decoys.
- **铁山 Tie Shan** — the heaviest. Heavy and charged slashes have super armor and take less damage; a huge guard.
- **凌霜 Ling Shuang** — long spear. Hits with the spear tip deal extra damage and launch. Spear Whirl knocks away projectiles.
- **流云 Liu Yun** — triple jump and fan dashes; the fan gives a strong guard. Hard to pin down.
- **雷鸣 Lei Ming** — six-hit punch chains and a quick-recovering guard. Vajra Armor absorbs a melee hit and calls lightning back.
- **墨羽 Mo Yu** — ink traps root the enemy in place. Built to break slow brawlers.
- **月华 Yue Hua** — Moon Mirror parries melee and always counters. Heals out of combat; waits, then strikes.
- **燕翎 Yan Ling** — fights in the air and avoids ground shockwaves, but her guard in the air is weaker.
- **玉箫 Yu Xiao** — basic hits store notes; Clear Tone spends them all, and more notes make a bigger, faster sound wave.
- **醉翁 Zui Weng** — every skill and hit fills his wine gourd. More wine means more damage, and past half a gourd he sometimes sways out of melee attacks. His ultimate traps the opponent inside a painting for a moment.
- **勾陈 Gou Chen** — chain hammer. Chain Hook pulls the enemy in, and a hammered enemy takes extra damage for a short time.
- **凌雀 Ling Que** — linked shuttles put Sparrow Marks on the target (up to 5); Shadow Swap homes in on the target.
- **离影 Li Ying** — leaves mist doubles of her soul around the stage and swaps to them. Heavier body, single jump.
- **磁仙 Ci Xian** — starts each round with a random magnetic pole and switches with `Q`. Positive hits launch; negative hits pull the enemy in and keep combos going.
- **千机 Qian Ji** — 金丝回环 (Silk Loop) keeps the puppet ball moving back and forth within a bounded tether. Your attacks can redirect and speed it up. 穿丝急袭 (Tether Lash) sends it through the aimed lane and breaks hitstun; 机轮三穿 (Gearwheel Triple Pass) makes three arena passes, then recalls the ball and refreshes `Q`.

## How A Match Works / 对局规则

- First to win **2 rounds** wins the match; a match ends after round **5** at the latest.
- In 3v3 relay the losing side brings in its next hero at full health; the winner keeps the health they had left plus a small heal.
- In 2v2, when your partner falls you get **孤军奋战 / Last Stand**: a full meter, a short power-up and some health back.

## Core Mechanics / 核心机制

| Mechanic | 机制 | How it works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Five-hit combo | 五连段 | Tap attack up to five times; the last hit usually launches or knocks back. |
| Charged slash | 蓄力斩 | Hold attack and release. Charging starts after about 0.3 s and is full at about 0.75 s. |
| Air attacks | 空中攻击 | Attack in the air for an air slash; attack while holding down for a slam. |
| Block and guard | 防御与架势 | Hold block to take only a little chip damage. Each blocked hit drains your guard; heavier attackers drain more. Guard refills when you stop blocking. |
| Guard break | 破防崩势 | When guard reaches zero you are stunned for a moment and open to a full combo. |
| Perfect parry | 精准招架 | Press block within about 0.09 s before a melee hit lands: no damage, the attacker is staggered, and you get some guard back. Projectiles and some special moves cannot be parried. |
| Escape | 腾空闪 | Right click or `Shift` with no attack held: a quick upward dodge out of pressure, on a cooldown that depends on the hero. |
| Weight | 体量 | Heavier heroes push lighter ones further and drain more guard; lighter heroes move faster. |
| Meter | 气 | Landing and taking hits fills the meter. A full meter lets you cast the ultimate, which plays a short close-up. |
| Projectiles | 暗器与剑气 | Many skills fire projectiles; some skills (Spear Whirl, Sword Qi, Qian Ji's ball) break or knock them away. |

## Frame Data / 帧数表

Startup, active and recovery frames for every normal attack, skill and ultimate are generated from the game code into [docs/ai/frame-data.md](https://github.com/AMFI-Disable/RenYing/blob/main/docs/ai/frame-data.md) (1 frame = 1/60 s). Run `node tools/checks/frame-data.mjs` after changing a move, and `node tools/checks/frame-data.mjs --check` to confirm timing did not change by accident.

每一招的前摇、判定、后摇帧数由代码生成在 [docs/ai/frame-data.md](https://github.com/AMFI-Disable/RenYing/blob/main/docs/ai/frame-data.md)。

## Where Hero Data Lives / 侠客数据在哪

One file per hero in [`src/data/heroes/`](https://github.com/AMFI-Disable/RenYing/tree/main/src/data/heroes): stats, balance multipliers, look, AI range, AI draft picks and both stories. The field reference is at the top of `src/data/00-heroes.js`, and `tools/checks/data-check.mjs` fails the build if a field is missing or wrong. Skill logic is in `src/game/03-skills.js` and ultimates in `src/game/04-ults.js`.
