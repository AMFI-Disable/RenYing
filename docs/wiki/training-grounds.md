# Training Grounds / 训练场

Training Grounds sits under **习武 → 训练场**, the Training category on the mode hall. It collects every practice activity, and every computer-vs-computer exhibition now lives here too, so the other mode cards only list matches you play yourself.

训练场在「习武 → 训练场」分类下，所有练习都收在这里；电脑对电脑的观战也全部搬到这里，其他模式卡只留自己上场的玩法。

## Activities / 训练项目

| Tab | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **招式教学 Move Lessons** | Guided steps on a straw dummy: movement, combos, school skills, guarding, precise parries, escape flashes and ultimates. Start with 开始完整教学 Start Full Tutorial. |
| **自由训练 Free Practice** | Pick 木人桩 Dummy or AI 实战对练 AI Sparring, then 开始自由训练 Start Free Practice. 点将选人开练 opens the draft if you want a different hero. |
| **情景训练 Scenario Drills** | Six focused drills, each set up with the right dummy behavior and auto-heal. |
| **实战演练 Match Practice** | Sends you to Play vs AI at the AI level you picked, for a full match. |
| **AI 观战 AI Watch** | Every AI vs AI exhibition, gathered in one list. |

### Scenario Drills / 六大专项课题

| Drill | What you practice |
| :--- | :--- |
| 连招与追击 Combos And Follow-ups | Openers, combos and follow-ups against a stationary target |
| 压制与破防 Pressure And Guard Breaks | Find openings against a target that keeps guarding |
| 精准招架 Precise Parries | Read close-range attacks and time the parry |
| 受压与脱身 Pressure And Escape | Defend against combos and escape flashes, then fight back |
| AI 实战拆招 AI Sparring | A real AI at your chosen level, with standard stats |
| 高台与空战应对 Platforms And Anti-Air | Intercept jumpers and chase them down after they land |

Each drill puts the dummy in position and turns on auto-heal, so you can repeat it without leaving the match.

每个课题会直接摆好木人的行为并开启回血，可以反复练，不用退出对局。

## Free Practice / 自由训练

### Dummy / 木人桩

The dummy has 300 health and is controlled from the 对手行为 Opponent Behavior bar: `Tab` opens it, or `F4` if you rebound `Tab`. Choose what it does, whether it repeats, how many seconds it waits between actions, and four traits.

木人桩 300 血，按 `Tab`（如果 Tab 已经改成别的键就按 `F4`）打开「对手行为」栏：选动作、是否重复、间隔几秒，还能开关四个特性。

Actions include 站桩 Stand, 来回走动 Walk, 近身普攻 Light Attack, 远程攻击 Ranged, 一直格挡 Always Guard, 精准招架 Precise Parry, 跳 Jump, 二段跳 Double Jump, 冲刺闪避 Dash, 技能 Q / 技能 R / 大招 E, 连招 Combo, 受击反击 Counter After Hit, 起身攻击 Wake-up Attack and 随机 Random.

动作包括站桩、来回走动、近身普攻、远程攻击、一直格挡、精准招架、跳、二段跳、冲刺闪避、技能 Q/R、大招、连招、受击反击、起身攻击和随机。

| Trait | Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| 钉在原地 Fixed | Cannot be knocked back or launched |
| 被连便闪 Escape On Combo | Escape-flashes after a launch or four hits |
| 坚若磐石 Infinite Guard | Guard never breaks |
| 连段自愈 Auto-Heal | Health refills when the combo stops |

### AI Sparring / AI 实战对练

AI sparring uses the level you pick (入门 / 寻常 / 劲敌 / 绝顶 / 天道) with **standard health and damage**. There is no stat inflation: a 天道 AI wins by reading you, not by having more health, so the habits you build here carry into real matches.

AI 实战对练按你选的境界（入门 / 寻常 / 劲敌 / 绝顶 / 天道）出手，血量和伤害都是标准值，没有数值膨胀：天道靠读招赢你，不是靠更厚的血，练出来的习惯可以直接带进正式对局。

## Training Tools / 训练工具

| Tool | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| 判定框 Hitboxes | `F1` toggles hitbox display. It is on by default in training and off in every real match. |
| 训练状态 Status Panel | Live health, guard, inner force, resources, cooldowns, combo damage, DPS, frame advantage, parries and guard breaks, in four tabs: 状态 Status, 徽记与灵宠 Badges & Pets, 战绩 Stats, 属性 Attributes. |
| 模拟频率 Simulation Rate | Slow the simulation from 120 Hz down to 1 Hz to look at one exchange. |
| 暂停时间 Pause Time | Freeze the match; with time paused, 推进 1 帧 Step One Frame advances one logic frame. |
| 满怒清冷 Full Meter | Refill inner force and clear skill and pet cooldowns. |
| 重置状态 Reset | Put both fighters, health, guard and statistics back to the start. |

The status panel and frame bar appear only in training; the hitbox view is forced off in every other mode.

训练状态面板和帧控制只在训练场出现；判定框在其他模式里会被强制关掉。

## AI Watch / AI 观战

AI Watch is the single place for computer-vs-computer matches. Pick the AI level in the bar above the list, then choose a card.

AI 观战是所有电脑对电脑对局的统一入口：先用上方「AI 境界」条选电脑强弱，再点一张卡片。

| Card | Match |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1v1 演武 Duel | Two AI heroes duel; it follows the Terrain Clash map vote if one is set |
| 3v3 车轮 Relay | Three-hero relay between two AI teams |
| 2v2 组队 Teams | Four AI heroes in two teams |
| 天机乱斗 Chaos | Random mutations every few seconds; the AI adapts |
| 墨球蹴鞠 Ink Ball | Two AI teams play the ink ball |
| 山河异境 Terrain Clash | AI teams fight on the terrain maps |
| 通天神决 Mayhem | Ten times the meter and no cooldowns |

Watch a few matches to study openers, spacing and how the AI covers its mistakes before you try the same mode yourself.

先看几局，研究起手、走位和电脑怎么补位，再自己去打同一个模式。
